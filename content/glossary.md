# Glossary

Terms used in Drake Stapleton's public project record. Follow the linked project pages for scope, evidence, and current status.

## AIEN and AIENOS

AIEN is the sovereign computing project described on [the AIEN page](/aien). AIENOS is its operating-system work; [aienos.com](https://www.aienos.com/) carries the current project record.

## Omega

Omega is the project's programming language and compute work. The [AIEN page](/aien) explains its place in the stack. A project goal is not proof that every language or hardware target is supported today.

## CUDA++

CUDA++ is described as the GPU half of Omega. The [CUDA++ page](/cudaplusplus) explains its implementation and links to the source.

## Atlas

Atlas is Drake's research program in accountable personal AI, continuity, training, and orchestration. See [why Atlas exists](/atlas).

## Atlas Symphony

An operator-led process for coordinating multiple AI models using bounded work lanes and evidence-bearing reviews. See [Symphony](/symphony) and its [workflow](/symphony/workflow).

## AEGIS

The defensive Atlas extension for authorized networks. See [AEGIS](/aegis) for its scope and evaluation record.

## Receipt and verification

A receipt is a record of a check associated with a specific change. A result applies to the recorded commit, test, and environment. See the [evidence hub](/evidence); do not treat a historical pass as proof about a later revision.

## Sovereign computing

The project's goal of owning and operating the machine, software, and intelligence. See [AIEN](/aien) and [research](/research) for the implementation and measured claims.
